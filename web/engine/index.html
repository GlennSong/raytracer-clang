The world

Building the ground

Mountains grown by uplift and erosion, rivers carved by drainage, maps that decide the cover, CDLOD drawing.

The road network

From a city plan to lanes, junctions, kerbs, signs and signals, up mountains and over rivers.

Buildings

The shape grammar and the architectural toolkit: masses, storeys, facades, and interiors you can walk.

Trees and forests

Lua recipes grown by C++, cards and impostors by distance, forests of hundreds of thousands.

Vehicles

Bodies from a mesh kit, Jolt vehicles under them, and the handling work that made them drive well.

Trains

Track in the lanes builder, the island loop, stations, light rail, and the people who ride.

Light, sky and weather

The physical renderer of November 2026: light in real units, a sky made by an atmosphere, reflections from probes and rays, and clouds placed by a simulated weather. A frame on the GPU shows how they fit into one frame.

Lights

One light system for every lamp, window and headlight, binned into clusters on the GPU; rooms lit from their fittings, and the light they bounce.

Sky, atmosphere and exposure

A physically based sky and the haze between you and the hills, the sky as a cubemap and spherical harmonics, and an eye that adapts.

Reflections

Probes captured along the streets and relit as the day turns, ray-traced reflections near the camera, and glass that tints.

Clouds

Volumetric clouds placed by a weather map, shaped by cloud types written as data, eroded into wisps, and casting shadows on the ground.

Weather and wind

Weather states as data, the wind, and a small fluid simulation of the air over the island that decides where clouds form.

Life in it

The living city

Hundreds of thousands of people with days, jobs and routes, simulated in tiers by distance.

Player mobility

The body as a state machine of modes: climbing, swimming, gliding, the grapple, boards, bikes, the driver's seat.

Sound made from nothing

Every sound synthesized: footsteps by surface, rivers from bubbles, engines and trains.
